Beachfront living

The Caribbean is waiting outside.

La Vida Belize sits on the Placencia Peninsula with the sea a few steps from your door. Spend the morning on the private beach, cool off in a pool, walk the pier, or take a kayak into clear water.

Accommodations are built with Belizean hardwoods from the nearby Maya Mountains. French doors open toward the sea, kitchens are fully equipped, and every room is air-conditioned.

01

Private beach & pier

A peaceful stretch of sand and a wooden pier with palapa — a favorite for sunrise, sunset and stargazing.

02

Pools

The Casa has a swimming pool and outdoor living deck. The Casita has a private plunge pool.

03

Kayaks & bikes

Complimentary tandem kayaks and bicycles for exploring the coast at your own pace.

04

Snorkel & fish

Practice snorkeling on coral heads just off the beach. Fishing is possible from the kayak, with tarpon across the road in the lagoon.

A day here

Let the light set the pace.

Sunrise coffee as the palms begin to move. Midday on the water or by the pool. Evening on the pier as the sky turns gold, then a short drive to Maya Beach or Placencia Village for dinner.

Many tour operators offer courtesy pick-up from La Vida, so reef trips, jungle days and village evenings are easy to arrange when you want them.
