The stay

Three private homes, one quiet beach.

Casa, the Casita, and the Studio are separate residences, built with Belizean hardwoods from the Maya Mountains, about twenty miles inland. They are not hotel rooms.

What they share is the beach — two lots — and a 75-foot pier with a palapa. The shore is still spacious enough for each stay to have its own stretch of sand.

On the grounds

Beach, pools, and the pier.

Step outside and the Caribbean is there. Casa and the Casita each keep their own pool. The beach, the pier, the kayaks, the bicycles, and the snorkel gear are for whoever is staying.

Day trips

Placencia, and farther out.

Placencia Village and Maya Beach are a short ride south, for dinner, groceries, and boats. From this part of the peninsula, guests go on to the Silk Cayes, Monkey River, and Bocawina. There are no photographs of those places in the set we were given, so none are shown here as if they were the property.

  • Maya Beach

    The nearer village stretch, a short ride from mile marker 11.

  • Placencia Village

    Restaurants, shops, and tour boats at the south end of the peninsula.

  • Silk Cayes

    A boat day on the barrier reef. Not photographed in this package.

  • Monkey River

    A river trip guests have taken during a stay. Not photographed here.

  • Bocawina

    Inland park and trails, west of the peninsula. Not photographed here.

Our story

A beach to come home to.

La Vida is three homes on one shore, close enough to Placencia for a night out and far enough that the evening belongs to the pier. Mornings can be a kayak, an afternoon a pool, and the night a chair under the palapa.

The grounds are looked after while you stay. Alyssa T wrote that groundskeeper Pablo was there for anything they needed. A host introduction will replace this note when it is ready.
